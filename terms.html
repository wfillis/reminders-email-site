Terms of service

Last updated: 11 October 2026

The service

Reminders Email is a personal macOS tool that reads incomplete items from the Apple Reminders app on the user's own Mac and sends them as email through the user's own Gmail account. It runs entirely on the user's Mac. There is no hosted service.

Use of the app

  • The user is responsible for the Gmail account and Google Cloud settings they connect, and for the recipient addresses they configure.
  • The app must be used in line with Google's terms of service and acceptable use rules, including Gmail's sending limits.
  • The app marks reminders as complete after it attempts to send them. The user accepts that a reminder can be completed even if its email later bounces or fails.

Google account access

The app asks for permission to send mail as the user. The user can revoke that permission at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. See the privacy policy for how data is handled.
